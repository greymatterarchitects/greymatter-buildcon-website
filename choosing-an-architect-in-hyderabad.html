How to choose an architect in Hyderabad

The best architect for your project is one whose experience and scope match what you need to build. Use these questions to compare firms before you appoint a design team for a home, workplace, hospitality project or institutional building.

1. Ask to see comparable work

Look for projects with a similar use, scale and level of complexity. Ask the firm what its team handled and which work reached construction. A concept can show how a firm thinks. A completed project can show how it resolved materials, details and coordination on site.

Greymatter’s selected work includes the High Court of Telangana proposal in Hyderabad and the completed Tribal Engineering College in Kurupam. The portfolio identifies each project’s location and stage.

2. Compare proposals by deliverable

Ask each firm to spell out what it will issue at concept, design development and construction drawing stages. Confirm who will coordinate structural and building-services consultants, prepare tender information, and respond to contractor questions. If you need interiors or project management, ask whether those are part of the proposal.

3. Discuss the site before committing to a design

Share the survey or plot dimensions, approach road, orientation, neighbouring buildings and any available site photographs. Ask how the team would investigate daylight, summer heat, rainwater drainage and access. Confirm who will identify the relevant approval requirements and what information they need from you.

4. Agree on fees, milestones and site involvement

Compare fee inclusions, consultant costs, revision allowances, payment milestones and the number of site visits. Ask who checks the drawings when site conditions differ from the survey, and who approves changes that affect cost or programme.

What to bring to a first consultation

  • Site location and any survey, dimensions or photographs you have.
  • Intended uses, key spaces, approximate floor area and priorities.
  • Working construction budget and target dates.
  • Any design, consultant coordination, tender or site support you need.
